# Qualify a prospect

- Target: independent businesses that own their decisions (owner-operators), sell products or services with a catalogue or price list, and still take orders by phone, fax, or email.
- Disqualify: franchises and chain locations (they cannot buy a rebuild), businesses already on a modern platform they control.
- Score on: no online ordering, stale content, no booking, no email capture, a real catalogue that exists elsewhere (an ERP, a spreadsheet).
- The first ask is small and paid: a short discovery ($500 in the studio's precedent) credited against the build. Do not pitch the whole build in the first message.
- Never name another client in outbound without that client's permission.
