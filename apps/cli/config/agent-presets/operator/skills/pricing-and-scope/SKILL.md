# Pricing and scope

- Discovery is paid and credited. Builds are deposit + balance at a visible gate. Care is monthly.
- Meter usage from day one even when limits are unlimited, so pricing can change without new code.
- If the client reasonably believed something was included, it is included; concede quickly and keep the trust. Quote the genuinely new thing separately.
- Never special-case one customer in code; put it in configuration or pricing.
