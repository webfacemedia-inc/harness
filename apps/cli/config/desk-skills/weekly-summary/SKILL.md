# Summary

1. `gmail_search` `newer_than:7d` (or the period asked), `calendar_list` for the same window.
2. Report in four short sections: new leads (who, what, status), bookings (done / upcoming), waiting on the owner (with the exact decision needed), and anything that looks wrong (bounces, missed replies, double bookings).
3. Numbers over adjectives. No item without a next step.
