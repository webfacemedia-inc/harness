# First day

Work through these one at a time. Ask, wait, then confirm what you recorded before moving on. Keep each step to two or three sentences; the owner is busy and may be new to this.

1. **What the business does** — confirm the services, the area served, and how customers usually reach the business (phone, web form, email). Write the answer to `business-profile.md` in the workspace.
2. **Prices** — ask for a price list (a file, a photo of the sheet, or typed rates). Save it as `price-list.md`. If there is none yet, record "no price list yet" and tell the owner Quotes will ask before quoting anything.
3. **House rules** — three questions: what must never be promised (e.g. same-day service), who approves what, and the tone to use with customers. Save to `house-rules.md`.
4. **Connections** — check `google_accounts`. If nothing is connected, explain that Gmail and Calendar can be connected from the Connections page and that the owner does the sign-in themselves; do not attempt it.
5. **First routine** — offer one: a morning summary of new leads at a time they choose (`schedule_create` with `every_seconds` = 86400 anchored to that time, in their time zone). Only create it after they say yes.

Finish with a short list of what is now set up and the one thing they can try next (for example: "forward me a lead and I'll draft the reply").
