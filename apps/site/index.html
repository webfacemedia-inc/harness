What it does

Not a chat window. An assistant that does the work, on a machine that belongs to you.

Always on, in Toronto

Desk runs on a private, always-on machine in Toronto with its own browser, files and schedule. Close your laptop; the work continues.

Signed in to your tools

Gmail, Calendar, Drive and Contacts through your own Google app; your website through webfaCeMEdia or WordPress; any other tool you add. You do the logins; Desk reuses them.

A browser you can watch

Desk has its own Chrome on its machine. Watch it work from your phone or laptop, and take the mouse whenever a site asks for a password or a code — Desk keeps the session afterwards.

Asks before it matters

Sending, booking, paying, publishing, deleting — each one stops for your approval, and your phone gets a notification when Desk is waiting on you.

Routines

"Every morning at 8, summarise new enquiries." Say it once; it runs on time, every time, and you can see and stop every routine from one page.

Add anything

Plug in any tool or service your business already uses, bring your own model account, or use ours.

What it handles

One assistant, four kinds of work. Switch between them in a click, or let Desk route the job itself.

Front desk

Answers every enquiry in your voice, sorts urgent from routine, gathers what's missing.

Q

Quotes

Prices only from your own price list and rules; clean quote documents ready to send.

B

Bookings

Books on your calendar, confirms, reminds, follows up; asks before moving anything.

+

Everything else

Files, research, documents, spreadsheets, your website, and any tool you connect.

Connects to what you already use

Every connection is your own account, set up once from Desk's Connections page — most in a few taps, Google in a guided five minutes.

webfaCeMEdia

If we built or run your website: sign in with your webfaCeMEdia account and Desk can update pages, draft campaigns, look up contacts and read your analytics — with your approval before anything goes live.

Google

Gmail, Calendar, Drive and Contacts through a Google app that belongs to your business, so your mail never passes through a shared app. Desk walks you through creating it, step by step.

WordPress

Pages, posts and media on your own WordPress site with an application password. New content lands as drafts; publishing waits for you.

Anything else

Add any tool server your business uses, or let Desk use its browser for the ones that have no connection yet.
