What's different about Desk

If you've run agents, automations or a chat tool with plugins, here is the part the homepage doesn't say. Desk is a full agent runtime on a machine of its own, with real tools, and you own every credential.

Its own machine

Each Desk is a private, always-on server in Toronto (DigitalOcean TOR1) with its own files, its own Chrome, and a persistent schedule. Close your laptop; it keeps working. Nightly snapshots, kept 30 days.

A browser you can watch

Desk drives a real Chrome on its machine. Watch it from your phone or laptop and take the mouse when a site wants a password or a code — Desk keeps the signed-in session afterwards.

Routines

"Every morning at 8, summarise new enquiries." Durable schedules, visible and stoppable from one page; each run still asks before anything leaves the business.

Connectors

Google (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Contacts) through a Google app that belongs to you — no shared app. webfaCeMEdia and WordPress built in. Any MCP server, by URL or command, from the Connections page.

Your model account

Bring your own OpenRouter key (DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi, Qwen, Anthropic, OpenAI) or use ours at cost +30%. A private-hardware option keeps inference on Canadian machines.

Guided or Full

Guided is read-only with approvals; Full adds file writes and a shell inside a sandbox, still with approvals on anything consequential. Modes without a shell can't reach one, even by delegation.

Approvals are fail-closed and logged; every conversation, tool call and approval is in the session log you can export. Sign-in is per Desk (password, phone sessions, or Sign in with Google). It's the same product the homepage sells — this is just the engineering view. Pricing is the same.
