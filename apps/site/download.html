webfaCe Desk on your computer

A window onto your Desk. Enter your Desk address once and it remembers you. Nothing runs on your computer — your Desk stays in Toronto; this is just the front door.

macOS

Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 12 or newer. One universal app.

First open

The first launch takes one extra click. After that it opens like any other app.

Mac

Unzip, move webfaCe Desk to Applications, then right-click → Open → Open once. The app clears the warning itself on that first run.

Windows

Unzip and run. If SmartScreen appears, choose More info → Run anyway.

Linux

Unzip and run the webfaCe Desk file. If it won't start, install libwebkit2gtk-4.0.

On your phone

There's nothing to download. Your Desk is a web app that installs from the browser and gets notifications.

  1. Open your Desk address (like yourname.webfacedesk.app) in Safari or Chrome.
  2. Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the Home Screen and tap Turn on notifications in the sidebar.

Why a web app on the phone?

It's the same Desk, kept in sync with your computer, with the browser view and approvals built in — and it updates itself without an app store.
