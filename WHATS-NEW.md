# What's new in webfaCe Desk

Newest first. One line per change, in the words an owner would use — this file is what the
update notice and the `/whats-new` page show, so no jargon and no version numbers.

## 29 August 2026
Updates now arrive without interrupting you: Desk tells you when a new version is ready, keeps your half-written message, and puts you back where you were.

## 29 August 2026
Files, quotes and decks open in a viewer with a way back, and downloads save properly from the desktop app.

## 29 August 2026
Desk can now make documents for you: quotes and letters as PDF or Word on your own letterhead, slide decks, spreadsheets and branded graphics. Set your logo and colours on the Business page.

## 28 August 2026
Sign in with Google, a Billing page, and the modes list is easier to move around.
